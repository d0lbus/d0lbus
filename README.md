## About Me

I am a BS Information Technology graduate from Saint Louis University, Philippines, focused on building software that connects **development, business operations, automation, and user experience**.

My work is not just about writing code. I care about understanding the real problem first, planning the workflow, organizing the system, and building tools that are useful for users, teams, and businesses.

I mainly work with **React, Laravel, Node.js, FastAPI, MySQL, and modern web tools**, while also developing my skills in **AI automation, n8n, Zapier, GoHighLevel, project management, client communication, product planning, and technical documentation**.

I enjoy building systems that make work easier, reduce manual tasks, organize information better, and help businesses operate more clearly.
